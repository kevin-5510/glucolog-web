Built from a real family experience

GlucoLog began when we saw how much work it took to manage prediabetes at home: checking blood sugar with a finger-prick meter, recording meals, and keeping up with daily activity. We wanted to make those routines easier to organize and understand.

Developed by Kelly Wang and Kevin Lu as a student technology project.

From tracking to understanding

Our long-term goal is to use machine learning to explore how eating habits, exercise, and other routines relate to glucose patterns, supporting more personalized everyday decisions.

Current web demo: records, summaries, and charts are functional. Personalized machine-learning predictions and medical recommendations are not included in this browser release.

Privacy by design

The web app stores records in this browser's local storage. No account or cloud database is required. Data does not automatically sync with the Android or desktop GlucoLog application.

Browser storage is not encrypted and may be erased when browsing data is cleared. Do not use this demo as the only copy of important medical records. Export your data regularly. Avoid shared devices.
